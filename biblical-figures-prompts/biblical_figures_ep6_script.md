- Matthew 11:2-6; Luke 7:18-23 (John's question from prison, Jesus's answer)
- Mark 6:21-29; Matthew 14:6-12 (the birthday banquet, the dance, and the beheading -- Mark does not name the daughter)
- Matthew 14:13 (Jesus withdraws on hearing the news)
- Medieval relic traditions: the Umayyad Mosque, Damascus; Amiens Cathedral, France (competing claims to hold John's head)
- Mandaean religious tradition (modern Iraq and Iran): John the Baptist venerated as their chief and final prophet
