- Acts 11:19-26 (Antioch, believers first called 'Christians')

- Acts 16 (Macedonian vision, Lydia, the Philippian jail and earthquake)
- Acts 17:16-34 (Athens, the Areopagus, 'an unknown god')
- Acts 18:1-18; 19:1-41 (Corinth, the Ephesus riot of Demetrius the silversmith)
- 1 & 2 Corinthians (letters written from Ephesus); 2 Corinthians 12:7-10 (the 'thorn in the flesh')
- 2 Corinthians 11:24-27 (Paul's own catalogue of sufferings)
- Acts 21:27-22:29 (the Temple riot, arrest, and his Roman citizenship)
- Acts 23:1-10 (before the Sanhedrin, Pharisees vs. Sadducees)
- Acts 24 (two years in custody under Felix at Caesarea)
- Acts 25-26 (Festus, Agrippa, and the appeal to Caesar)
- Acts 27-28 (the shipwreck off Malta, the viper, and two years of house arrest in Rome)
- Eusebius, Church History 2.25 (early tradition of Paul's beheading under Nero and burial on the Via Ostiense)
- 1 Clement 5 (c. 96 AD, an early reference pointing to Paul's martyrdom)
- Modern scholarly consensus on seven undisputed Pauline letters versus six disputed (the Pastorals among them) -- the same authorship debate noted for 1 and 2 Peter in the previous episode
