TITLE:
Caiaphas: The High Priest Who Outlasted Pilate

DESCRIPTION (dan len YouTube):
Rome removed high priests every few years. Caiaphas held the office for eighteen years, long enough to preside over the night hearing of Jesus.

We follow Caiaphas from the Roman system that made and unmade high priests, through his family ties to Annas, the council that decided one man's death was cheaper than a nation's, the night hearing and the handover to Pilate, his sudden removal in the year 36 -- and the 1990 discovery of a carved bone box inscribed "Joseph son of Caiaphas."

We separate what the Gospels and the historian Josephus record from the later tradition built on top of them, and look at the two ways historians read his motives.
